# Changelog

What changed in each version of Dream Download Manager, newest first.
Dream DM shows these in "What's new" and after it updates itself.

How to write an entry (one section per release, before tagging it):

- 1 to 3 short lines, in plain words, about what people will notice.
- Start a line with ★ if it's a highlight: someone who skipped many
  updates only sees the highlights of older versions.

## 0.9.6
- ★ Delete a finished download's file from Dream DM: ✕ now offers "Remove and delete the file" or "Delete the file only" (it goes to the Recycle Bin).
- Files moved or deleted outside Dream DM are marked "File not found", with Download again.
- What's new now also shows after updating from 0.9.4 or older.

## 0.9.5
- ★ "What's new" shows what changed since your version, inside Dream DM.
- After an update, Dream DM shows a short summary of what's new.

## 0.9.4
- Fixed: the Update button in Settings was hard to read.

## 0.9.3
- ★ Dream DM can update itself: Update now installs the new version and reopens.
- An Update button in Settings > Dream DM updates.

## 0.9.1
- ★ Video look-ups are much faster while something is downloading.
- Optional: video links you copy are looked up straight away (Settings > Catching links).
- The video picker shows what it's doing while it looks a video up.

## 0.9.0
- ★ The first release: fast downloads that resume, video downloads, browser extension, Windows and Linux.
